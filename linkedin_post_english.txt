Is the traditional four-year degree still the ultimate benchmark of success in today's dynamic world? While foundational knowledge remains crucial, the landscape of "education" is rapidly expanding beyond university halls.

We're seeing a powerful shift towards continuous learning, practical skill acquisition, and micro-credentials. Online courses, bootcamps, apprenticeships, and even self-directed learning through open resources are proving incredibly valuable. It's less about a single destination and more about a lifelong journey of acquiring relevant skills and adapting to new challenges.

Ultimately, education in the 21st century seems to be about cultivating curiosity and the ability to learn, unlearn, and relearn. What does "educated" truly mean to you in this evolving era?
