It's a conversation many of us tiptoe around, yet its presence is felt deeply in our communities and workplaces: depression. Far from just "feeling sad," it's a complex health condition that impacts millions globally, affecting energy, focus, and overall well-being.

Recognizing depression as a legitimate illness, rather than a personal failing, is the first crucial step towards fostering environments where individuals feel safe to seek help and receive support. Empathy, understanding, and open dialogue can significantly reduce the stigma that often prevents people from getting the care they need.
